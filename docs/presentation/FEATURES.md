# ✨ ALUMNIBRIDGE: COMPLETE FEATURE INVENTORY

### 1. Student Experience
- **Guided 8-Step Career Onboarding:** Collects education, target roles, desired industries, technical skills, and mentorship preferences.
- **Dynamic Student Command Center:** Displays real-time Career Readiness Index, recommended next action, active mentorship summary, and recommended mentors.
- **Transparent Mentor Discovery:** Real-time search with multi-faceted filtering (industry, role, skills, availability, mode) and explainable match scores.
- **Structured Mentorship Request:** Formulates SMART mentorship goals, expectations, and meeting cadences.
- **Collaborative Mentorship Workspace:** Manage shared milestones, view session agendas, and submit evidence links.
- **Signature Career Journey Timeline:** Visual timeline mapping key career milestones from profile creation to verified job offers.
- **Career Outcome Registration:** Student records employment, internship, or higher education placement.

### 2. Alumni / Mentor Experience
- **Alumni Mentorship Dashboard:** Pending mentee requests, active mentees, upcoming sessions, and recent activity.
- **One-Click Request Review:** Accept or decline incoming mentorship requests with feedback.
- **Milestone & Evidence Verification:** Review student deliverable evidence (PRs, live demos, Figma links) and mark verified.
- **Signature "My Impact" Dashboard:** Quantified volunteer impact metrics: Students Mentored, Sessions Held, Goals Verified, and Referrals.

### 3. Institution Administration & Career Team Experience
- **Macro Institutional Dashboard:** Active mentorships, goal completion rates, verified outcomes, and engagement funnels.
- **Operational Early Intervention Module:** Support alerts for students requiring attention (overdue goals, inactive mentorships, incomplete profiles).
- **Skill Gap & Placement Analytics:** Real-time demand vs. supply comparisons across academic departments and industries.
- **One-Click CSV Export:** Comprehensive institutional data download for reporting and accreditation.
- **Curated Career Resource Library:** Institutional guides, interview frameworks, resume checklists, and technical roadmaps.

### 4. Judge Mode
- **Instant Persona Switcher:** One-click role switching between Student, Mentor, Admin, and Career Team without typing credentials.
